GENOMES NATIVE THIRD-PARTY REGISTER
===================================

The native build keeps third-party source revisions explicit.  The parent
repository will carry gitlinks under external/ once a dependency is adopted.
Until then, the dependency remains disabled by default and no configure step
downloads it.
