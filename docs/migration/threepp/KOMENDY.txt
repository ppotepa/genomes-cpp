F. VISUAL REVIEW CHECKLIST
--------------------------
Compare the captures with the JS reference/prototype and inspect:
  - silhouette and overall proportions
  - shoulder/torso-arm transition
  - elbow/knee continuity in run/crouch
  - hand scale, fingers and wrist transition
  - eye opening and eyelid closure direction
  - nose/lips/ears/jaw silhouette
  - neck/head deformation
  - scalp coverage, temple recession, widow peak and crown volume
  - hair from front/side/back
  - separation of skin / cloth / hair / leather / metal / painted equipment
  - key light from above, no inverted/below-ground character lighting
  - contact shadows without obvious clipping at the active camera target
  - no NaN/degenerate geometry artifacts
