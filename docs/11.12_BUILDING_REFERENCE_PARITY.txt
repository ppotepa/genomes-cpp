======================================================================
PUBLIC CONTRACTS
======================================================================
1. Fixture corpus covers rectangle/L/U/irregular footprints, 1..multi storeys,
family/apartment/factory/public-like programs where SOURCE supports, multiple
roofs, high/low regularity/symmetry, stair/core edge cases and regression seeds.

It MUST also cover every conflict recorded by Chapter 11.1 between SOURCE
buildingCatalog/buildingSpec/buildingPrograms. In particular RURAL_SHED size
semantics require separate explicit fixture/provenance rather than one guessed
range.

2. Compare TARGET invariants: footprint validity, room access, stair fit, roof
quality, no overlap/opening errors, deterministic IDs and collision/nav
consistency. SOURCE-specific IDs or numeric outputs are diagnostic only.

3. Useful failure cases from SOURCE tests and building-lab are translated to a
TARGET requirement/test or explicitly rejected as irrelevant; they never create
a legacy runtime mode.

4. Visual geometry may improve, but semantic openings/rooms/roof/structure must remain internally consistent.

5. The initial TARGET validation corpus covers at least seven programs/presets,
ten footprint families including EXPLICIT, seven roof families, four stair
families and all six useful building-lab scenario classes. These are minimum
coverage requirements, not frozen SOURCE catalogs.

6. Research concepts such as additional families/roofs, kitchens, bathrooms and
lifts enter the final product scope only when an owning generator contract,
validator and acceptance case are added. They are not empty compatibility flags.

    plus known regressions and every SOURCE definition conflict from 11.1.

======================================================================

    nativePlan := generate(input)
    validate semantic invariants

    compare expected metrics/tolerances
    record result
