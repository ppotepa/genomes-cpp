======================================================================
PUBLIC CONTRACTS
======================================================================
1. RoadCostField samples positive traversal multiplier/cost per raster/cell from slope, water, protected/reserved masks, urban attraction and road class.

2. A* search domain grid/graph is explicit. Heuristic = Euclidean distance * global minimum per-meter cost for admissibility.

3. Bridge-capable major classes may cross water with high/bridge construction cost; local classes may treat water impassable unless crossing candidate exists.

4. Router returns a RoadRoute containing:
    raw routed polyline,
    smoothed RoadCenterline candidate,
    cost,
    bridge-crossing annotations.

Smoothing may use Chaikin/polyline fitting or cubic Bézier fitting but:
    - start/end must remain exactly the route endpoint node coordinates,
    - it cannot cross a forbidden cell/water policy not present in raw route,
    - maximum deviation/error is explicit,
    - topology/collision tests use adaptive centerline sampling where required.

IMPLEMENTATION STEPS
======================================================================
STEP 1
    Build aligned RoadCostFieldArtifact from terrain slope/hydrology and urbanity inputs.

STEP 2
    Implement grid A* with binary heap, gScore arrays and visit generation stamps to avoid clearing huge arrays every route.

STEP 3
    Use deterministic priority tuple (f,g,nodeStableIndex) for ties.

STEP 4
    Reconstruct path, simplify line-of-sight/cost-aware only when every replacement segment respects field constraints.

STEP 5
    Annotate water crossing intervals for later bridge edge creation.

STEP 6
    Benchmark route batches and reuse search buffers through scratch/context.

======================================================================
PSEUDOCODE
======================================================================
A*:
    g[start]=0
    push(start, f=h(start), tie=startIndex)
    while open:
        cur := popMin()
        if cur == goal: reconstruct
        for neighbor in neighbors(cur) stable order:
            step := distance*cost(neighbor,class)
            tentative := g[cur]+step
            if tentative < g[neighbor]:
                came[neighbor]=cur
                g[neighbor]=tentative
                f=tentative+h(neighbor)
                push/update
    return NoPath
